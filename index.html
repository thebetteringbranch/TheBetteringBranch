WHO WE ARE

The Bettering Branch is a youth-led non-profit focused on empowering youths with empathy through connection, education and service. We do this one branch at a time with our 3 main branches of Planet, People & Advocacy.

OUR MISSION

We believe empathy can make the world go round!

So we hope to lead & champion it amongst Singaporean youths to dismantle the 'checkbox' culture of youth volunteerism in Singapore & lowering the barriers to civic engagement through connnection, education & action.

We aspire to be a platform where youths can come together to connect, empathise, lead.

OUR STORY SO FAR...

Back in school, the phrase "Young people are the future" was something we heard almost daily. Yet, around us, the reality was very different. Everyone seemed indifferent. Conversations about social issues were zero to none, whilst talks of video games, academics or the latest trend dominated. And if any issues were ever discussed, it would be followed shortly by the terms 'For my portfolio/DSA/ABA', 'Compulsory' & 'LEAPS Points'. In other words, volunteerism, concern and any action towards these social and environmental issues were boxes to tick, not born out of a real want to change things. But this was just a sympton of an even larger problem. The growing apathy in young Singaporeans.

On paper and from the outside, young Singaporeans look engaged. Hundreds of hours are recorded, and projects are completed. But beneath that, if you were to sit in the canteen and join the daily conversations, you'll realise the apathy is easily identifiable. Its as simple as not even batting an eye when food is wasted or faking attention when real social issues are taught in school. It sounds pretty awful, but here's the thing. We never blamed our peers for any of it.

With exams that never seem to end, 7am to 7pm school days (days with CCA trainings), and the woes of being a teenager, where could Singaporean youths find the time to breathe, let alone care about the issues of today?

We (the founders) empathised with this, because we lived it. And we weren't special either, in a way that we were wired differently from other Singaporean youths to the point where the widespread apathy didn't affect us. We were only 'special' because we were lucky enough to have the opportunity to truly connect with the issues. And so, with the knowledge that our peers weren't inherently 'bad', but just never had the same opportunities we had, we looked for platforms to give them the opportunities.

We couldn't find any. And so, we built it.

In January 2024, with no funding, mentors or any institutional support, driven solely by conviction and a dream, The Bettering Branch was born. We set out with cold emails and texts, online forums and a vision. A vision of an organisation that allowed Singaporean youths (like ourselves) to give back, through not just flexible & accessible opportunities that lower the barriers to civic engagement, but activities that allowed youths to genuinely connect.

Since then, we have only grown exponentially, surpassing our own expectations and goals. As of our last count, we have mobilised more than 600 volunteers, been awarded the Victoria Pioneer Award & impacted more than 1,000 seniors, kids & communities at risk in just 2 years! We look forward to what 2026 brings & to continue branching out for the better!

OUR THEORY OF CHANGE

No lectures or guilt, but repeated exposure and genuine camaraderie.

We lower the barriers to showing up by not just making our event dates flexible and tailored to the busy schedules of Singaporean youths, but we also structure them so that giving back feels as enjoyable and energising as hanging out with your friends or doing something you love.

From there, we see youths return for our events again and again. And yes, maybe its because the flexibility and fun makes it an 'easy' way to get hours, but what really matters to us, is that they keep showing up. Because the more they show up, the more they feel it.

Every event is an experience where youths are given the opportunity and space to connect. We cannot manufacture the moment it clicks, but we strive to create the conditions for it to happen every time, until one day, giving back and actually caring feels like second nature.

Our theory of change in short is to get Singaporean youths to keep showing up because they innately want to, and from there, we foster connections until apathy is seen as almost illogical, and empathy as simply the way to be.

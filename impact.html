SDG 3: Good Health & Well-Being

- The Bettering Run combined physical activity with educational outreach, promoting peer-driven health advocacy for more than 200 participants that showed up
- Our Fengshan CC Luncheons incorporate activities that support cognitive resillience and agility in seniors and has impacted more than 500 seniors since 2024

SDG 4: Quality Education

- Through Mission Lighthouse, our weekly befriending & academic tutoring programme for primary school students (aged 7–12), we work towards providing equitable learning access and resources to support the social, motor & cognitive development of more than 100 children
- Our infographic posts on Instagram about topics like food waste or practising sustainability during Valentine's Day have provided an accessible and equitable place for youths to learn more about important social issues & what they can do to help. The posts have reached over 200,000 views combined.

SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

- We have spent more than 40 hours delivering food to homebound seniors, ensuring seniors get nutrious meals despite being physically immobile (Partnering with Bethesda Care Services)
- We raised more than 2,600SGD via our Street Sales Fundraiser for SHINE Children & Youth Services, supporting disadvantaged youths (Collaboration with Project Joey)

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption & Production

- Through advocacy at events like The Wasteless Utopia, we educate attendees on sustainable fashion/living policies, advocating for responsible consumption habits.
- Recycle Red!, our nation-wide initiative mobilised 30 schools (≈ 100K+ Students, using data.gov.sg Full-Time Polytechnic, Secondary School & Junior College students statistics) in 2026, and diverted 435KG worth of used red paper packets from landfills in 2025. That's estimated to be about 108,750 red packets recycled, preventing roughly 522kilograms of CO2 emissions (assuming 1kg of paper saves ≈1.2kg CO2 emissions)
- Our seniors' luncheons incorporate sustainability through activity workshops that use recyclable materials!
- Event decor from our event, The Bettering Run was 90% sourced second-hand or made from recycled materials


SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

Through partnerships with more than 60 schools, organisations and youth-led initiatives/projects , we leverage cross-sector collaboration to scale impact, strengthen community networks & drive integrated social and environmental change!
- Our partners range from local youth initiatives like YMCA YFC's Project Joey to People's Association and the Sustainable Singapore Gallery, as well as international youth organisations like Pangea Kids Burbank California

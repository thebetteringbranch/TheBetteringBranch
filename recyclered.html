WHAT IS RECYCLE RED!?

Recycle Red! is a Chinese New Year sustainability initiative by The Bettering Branch, seeking to promote sustainable & eco-conscious habits during Chinese New Year by partnering with Geneco to bring Red Packets Recycling bins to Schools.

WHY CAN'T WE RECYCLE RED PACKETS IN NORMAL RECYCLING BINS?

1. Metallic foil and gold stamping
- Red packets contain metallic foil and metals that can contaminate the paper recycling process.
- When paper is recycled, it gets pulped in water
= the foil doesn't break down in this process and instead clogs the machinery and contaminates the resulting pulp batch.
2. Red dye
- Heavy red dye can also be a problem for many paper recyclers as it complicates the recycling process, making pulping more labour intensive & costly
- The dye can bleed into the process water, requiring more frequent water changes & reducing water reusability too!
3. Decorative plastic coatings
- Plastic coated paper cannot be pulped the same way as plain paper, which makes it challenging for typical recycling bins to collect them

HOW RECYCLE RED! BEGAN

Rooted in the legend of Sui, a demon said to harm sleeping children, the Chinese New Year (CNY) tradition of giving red packets has always centred youths as its primary recipients. And so, every CNY, millions of red packets are given to youths all across Singapore. Not shortly after, when the festivities end, millions of red packets are then also thrown away by youths all across Singapore.

Geneco had already recognised this problem. In 2021, they launched their Used Red Packet Recycling initiative, placing collection bins across malls in Singapore. But there was a bit of a friction problem. The primary recipients of the red packets, students & youths, were not only unaware that initiatives like this existed, they were also not willing to go to the trouble of bringing their red packets to a mall, when tossing them in their bins at home was much easier.

So we (The Bettering Branch) decided to do something about it. We decided to bring the red packets to where almost all youths go to every single day, rain or shine. School!

In December 2024, our founder reached out to Geneco to pitch an expansion of their existing initiaive into schools, & thus Recycle Red! was born.

Frequently Asked Questions


Why can't red packets go into normal recycling bins?

Red packets have three features that make them incompatible with standard paper recycling: metallic foil & gold stamping clog machinery and contaminate paper pulp; heavy red dye bleeds into process water, making recycling more costly; and plastic coatings on some packets can't be pulped the same way as plain paper. Recycle Red! bins work with Recycle SG who have specialised processes to handle these materials.


How do I get my school involved

Reach out to us at thebetteringbranch@gmail.com or via our social media. We welcome Junior Colleges, secondary schools, & other educational institutions.


What happens to the red packets after they're collected?

Collected red packets are passed to SG Recycle, who process them through a specialised recycling stream designed to handle the metallic foils, dyes, and coatings. This prevents them from ending up in landfills, where paper produces methane as it decomposes & reduces the need for energy-intensive virgin paper pulp production.

WHAT IS RECYCLE RED!?

Recycle Red! is a Chinese New Year sustainability initiative by The Bettering Branch, seeking to promote sustainable & eco-conscious habits during Chinese New Year by partnering with Geneco to bring Red Packets Recycling bins to Schools.

THE STORY OF RECYCLE RED!

In December of 2024, our founder

n 2021, Geneco started the Used Red Packet Recycling Initiative to collect used and excess red packets via bins placed in shopping malls. Through the past 5 consecutive years, the initiative has seen growing participation from the public. In December of 2024, our founder In 2025, We reached out to Geneco and started Recycle Red!, a nationwide Chinese New Year sustainability initiative which seeks to promote sustainable & eco-conscious habits during Chinese New Year by partnering with Geneco SG to bring Red Packet Recycling bins to Schools. Recycle Red! is also an inter-school competition, to motivate & reward youths that choose sustainability.

In 2025, we partnered with 13 Junior Colleges to recycle a total of 435 Kilograms worth of red packets. That's over 100K red packets! (assuming 1 red packet weighs 5-8g) Furthermore, Anderson Serangoon Junior College (ASRJC), recycled 80 kilograms worth of red packets & won a visit from the Milo Van. We thank the Milo Van, Geneco and all our partners for making Recycle Red! 2025 a success!

In 2026, Recycle Red! will unite almost all Pre-University schools in Singapore & an assortment of secondary schools to join the initiative! Because red packets often have heavy ink, foil stamping, or special materials, they cannot be recycled in standard, single-stream blue bins.
